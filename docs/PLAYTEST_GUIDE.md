# Friend Playtest Guide

## Start here

Play the public build: <https://dumb-tony.github.io/pest-control/>

The game takes about 5–10 minutes. Please play once without reading the strategy section below. The prototype works with a mouse or touch; keyboard shortcuts are shown at the bottom of the screen.

## What to send back

Copy the job link before or after playing so the exact seed can be repeated. Then answer these five questions:

1. Which animal did you diagnose, and which clues convinced you?
2. Before starting containment, where did you expect the animal to move?
3. If something went wrong, could you tell what caused it?
4. Did you notice that you could place an emergency barrier during containment?
5. Did capture, repair, cleanup, and the final job report feel like one complete job?

Also include the final grade, damage, trust, job value, device/browser, and anything that felt confusing or unfair.

## Controls

- Click/tap evidence markers and rooms.
- `1`: inspect
- `2`: live trap
- `3`: temporary barrier
- `4`: exclusion patch
- `5`: HEPA cleanup
- `Space`: start containment
- `R`: restart the same seeded job

During preparation, click placed equipment again to remove and refund it. A live trap can be moved without paying twice.
